## 3. Preregistered utility gate

A result may be reported as *detection of structural transition dynamics* on discrimination evidence alone (AUROC/AUPRC vs comparator).

A result may **only** be reported as a candidate for clinical deployment if, in out-of-sample validation at the setting's true prevalence, it achieves all of:

- PPV ≥ 0.20 (NNA ≤ 5), **and**
- alert rate ≤ 1 false alert per patient per observation horizon, **and**
- median lead time ≥ 2 h (ICU) or ≥ 7 d (psychiatric), **and**
- calibration slope in [0.8, 1.25] out of sample.

Section 2 shows this gate implies specificity in the 0.99+ region for every setting except event-rich ICU circulatory failure. No published early-warning system in these domains currently clears it.


## 4. Reference point

The Epic Sepsis Model row is not hypothetical: sens 0.33, spec 0.83, AUROC 0.63, PPV 0.12 in external validation on 38,455 hospitalisations (Wong et al., *JAMA Intern Med* 2021), while alerting on 18% of all hospitalised patients. It is included as the empirical floor that any Transition Dynamics estimator must beat before it is discussed further.
